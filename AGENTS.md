# AGENTS.md

The rulebook for this operating system. Read it first, every session.

## How this system works

All lasting knowledge lives in `wiki/`. `CURRENT.md` says what is true right now. `SESSION_LOG.md` is history.

## Every session

1. Read this file, then `CURRENT.md`.
2. Open `wiki/index.md` to find the page for what the owner wants to work on.
3. Ask the owner what they want to work on.
4. At the end, update `SESSION_LOG.md`, then bring `CURRENT.md` up to date. Replace what changed. Keep history out of it.

## Rules

1. **The wiki is the source of truth.** When your memory and the wiki disagree, the wiki wins.
2. **Log every wiki change** as one line in `wiki/log.md`.
3. **A decision gets its own dated page** in `wiki/decisions/`, with the owner's words quoted. A decision page is never edited afterwards. A new decision replaces it.
4. **When the owner corrects you, write it down** as a page in `wiki/feedback/`, and update every page the correction touches.
5. **Ask where a new file should live** before creating it.
6. **Use the simplest approach that works.**
